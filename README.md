🔹 What is ConnectEach?
 ConnectEach is a professional networking platform where users can:
 • Create a professional profile page to showcase their skills, experience, and projects
 • Connect with other professionals and developers
 • Create teams to collaborate on projects

🔹The idea behind ConnectEach is to bridge the gap between individuals who want to showcase their skills and those who want to collaborate on meaningful projects.

 Frontend:
 •Next.js (React)
 Backend:

 •Express.js
 Database:
